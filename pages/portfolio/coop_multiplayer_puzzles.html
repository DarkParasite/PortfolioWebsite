Description and Showcase

I wanted to learn Unreal Engine's Replication System, and at the time I played It Takes Two and I loved that game so much. Decided to make some of my own co-op mechanics to get familiar with multiplayer programming in Unreal Engine. Not my best multiplayer project, but I managed to implement some mechanics successfully.

Key Features and Mechanics

  • Coop Open Door
  • Replicated Rail-Cart driven by one Player
  • Moving Platform Puzzles
  • Color Matching Giant structure Puzzle
