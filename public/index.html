Who Am I?

Co-Founder and Lead Programmer at Aeos Games. Self-taught, insanely passionate Game Programmer, with over 6 years of experience making games in Unreal Engine. Started my game dev journey in 12th grade, in college I freelanced on Fiverr, working with clients worldwide.

I'm on the path to mastering the art of making and designing games. I value game development and games more than anything, I consider it the highest form of art, where you can present different perspectives, virtues, and knowledge as an experience.

I get high from designing and building complex systems that are beautifully decoupled and does their job like a machine. C++ forever. It’s like a Katana that demands form and control, but in the hands of a Kensei, it turns the blood of his enemies into paint and the battlefield as the canvas.
